How to Play

  • Place a String: On your turn, stretch one string between any two of the 8 edge pegs (all 28 chords, including the outer octagon edges, are playable once).
  • Claim Territory: Closing an area completely bounded by your strings floods those interior stone wells with your color — unless an opponent's string or claimed well lies inside.
  • Territory Lock: You cannot place a chord that crosses through an opponent's claimed area. Crossing your own claimed area is allowed.
  • Winning: If you have no legal move, you pass. The game ends automatically when no player can move or no scoring moves remain; whoever owns the most individual stone wells wins.
